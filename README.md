# Apex Digital AI 2.0

Fully autonomous AI design studio, white-label agencies, marketplace, subscription & credits, video/motion/ads generation, enterprise compliance, and autonomous sales & growth AI.

- AI Project Manager
- Logo, Website, Marketing, Video, Motion, Ads generation
- Credit-based subscription billing
- Multi-agency white-label support
- Global marketplace with reviews and transactions
- Enterprise compliance (RBAC, audit logs, encryption)
- Autonomous growth AI (lead generation, upselling, marketing)
- PWA-ready frontend & Windows installer
